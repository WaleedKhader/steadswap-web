Home exchange for pet owners

Your pets stay home, cared for by someone like you — and you travel free.

Stead is a home exchange between pet owners. You stay in another member's home and look after their animals. They stay in yours and look after yours.

Who it's for

People with a cat or a dog who want to travel, and who would rather not put their animal in a kennel or hand a key to a stranger who has never owned one.

Every swap is between two verified animal owners — that is the condition for confirming one, not a preference on a listing. So the person feeding your cat at seven in the morning already knows what that morning looks like.

How it works

  1. 1

    Get verified

    Four checks: who you are, that the home is yours, that the animals are yours, and the care pledge. The three that need evidence are read by a person, not a machine — and sending a document is not passing a check.

  2. 2

    Find a home that fits

    Dates that overlap, a place you want to go, and animals you are able to look after. Browsing, listing your home and asking for a swap are free.

  3. 3

    Swap

    You stay in their home and care for their animals. They stay in yours and care for yours. A swap can only be confirmed when all four checks are green on both sides.

The difference is an obligation, not a filter

Other home exchanges have a pet-friendly setting — a preference on a listing saying an animal is allowed in the house. On Stead a swap can only be confirmed between two verified animal owners, and caring for the other household's animals is the exchange itself rather than a favour attached to it.

  • Both sides, all four checks

    Identity, home, pet ownership and the care pledge — all four, on both sides, before a swap can be confirmed. The documents are read by a person, and the app never shows a tick for one nobody has read.

  • Your address stays private

    Your street address is withheld by the database, not by the interface. A member who is not your confirmed counterparty cannot read it, whatever the app asks for.

  • Host 1 night = travel 1 night

    That's it — that is the whole credit formula. Nothing to convert, and no points jargon to learn.

  • The paywall is at the handshake

    Browsing, listing a home, adding pets, verifying, asking for a swap and messaging a host are free and stay free. A membership is what turns an accepted swap into a confirmed one.

  • Nothing sends itself

    Four features use AI, each labelled where it appears. Every one produces a draft you approve, edit or throw away. No decision about your account is made by a machine alone.

  • Talk to a person

    Support is a person, not a bot and not a help centre. Stead is run by one person, so you are writing to them directly.

Where things stand
